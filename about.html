Platform Overview

Dental Practice Management & Clinic Discovery

DentalSmart is a web-based dental software platform designed for dental clinics and dental teams. It connects essential day-to-day clinic operations with online patient scheduling and clinic discovery.

View Plans & Pricing Browse Clinic Directory

1. Practice Operations

DentalSmart provides a central workspace to manage daily clinic workflows, replacing fragmented spreadsheets and manual registers with structured digital tools:

Appointment Calendar

Schedule and review upcoming appointments, monitor daily queues, and record arrival and attendance status.

Patient Records

Maintain centralized patient profiles with contact details, visit history, clinical notes, and allergies. Online bookings automatically link to existing patients or create a new profile when needed.

Treatment Logs

Record procedure descriptions, tooth numbers, clinical notes, prescriptions, and attachments for each visit.

Billing & Invoices

Generate itemized invoices for dental treatments, track payment status, apply taxes or discounts, and view outstanding balances.

2. Clinic Discovery & Online Booking

DentalSmart connects internal management with external patient discovery, giving dental practices tools to accept bookings 24/7:

Direct Booking Link

Every clinic receives a dedicated public booking link to place on Google Maps listings, website buttons, or patient communications, allowing patients to schedule visits directly.

Public Clinic Directory

Patients seeking dental care can browse participating clinics through the DentalSmart directory (/clinics), check working hours, and request appointments.

Plan Inclusions Note: Direct online booking links, calendar scheduling, patient records, treatment logs, and billing are core platform capabilities. Inclusion in the central public marketplace directory and automated appointment reminders are available on select subscription plans (Growth and Premium tiers) or during the active 3-month free trial period. Specific plan details and limits are outlined on our Plans & Pricing page.

3. Delivery & Architecture

DentalSmart is delivered as a cloud-hosted web application:

No Local Installation

Access the platform from desktop, laptop, or tablet browsers without installing or maintaining a local application server.

Role-Based Permissions

Support for clinic administrators, associate dentists, and front-desk receptionists with role-specific views and permission boundaries.

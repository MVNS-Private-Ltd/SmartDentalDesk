How DentalSmart Automated Reminders Work

Missed appointments lead to empty treatment chairs and disrupted clinic schedules. DentalSmart runs an automated background reminder job every 24 hours that scans for upcoming scheduled visits:

24-Hour Reminder Window

The scheduler identifies appointments scheduled between 23 and 25 hours ahead that have not yet received a notice, ensuring timely delivery a day before the visit.

Patient Email Delivery

Each patient with a valid email address on file receives a structured notification containing the appointment date, scheduled time, clinic name, and clinic contact details.

Automatic Deduplication

The job checks previous message logs to prevent duplicate emails from being dispatched for the same appointment, ensuring patients receive only one notice per visit.

Campaign Log Auditing

Every dispatch attempt is recorded in your clinic's message log with the recipient, timestamp, and delivery status for administrative tracking.

Plan Availability & Channel Boundaries

To ensure reliable email deliverability, automated appointment reminders are available on select subscription tiers:

Included Tiers

Automated reminders are active on the Growth and Premium subscription tiers, as well as during the active 3-month free trial.

Starter Plan Scope

Clinics on the Starter tier manage appointments manually through the calendar and can communicate with patients via their own clinic phone or email channels.

Operational Boundaries: DentalSmart automated appointment reminders operate strictly through the email channel. DentalSmart does not offer an automated WhatsApp Cloud/Business API integration (though clinics may share their public booking link manually via WhatsApp web links), does not provide SMS gateway delivery, and does not process incoming patient confirmation replies (e.g. automated two-way "Reply 1 to confirm" parsing). Reminders help patients remember upcoming appointments but do not guarantee zero cancellations or 100% attendance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What information is required for a patient to receive a reminder?

The patient's profile or appointment booking must include a valid email address. If no email is provided, the reminder is skipped and logged accordingly.

Can we send WhatsApp reminders automatically?

No. DentalSmart does not have an automated WhatsApp messaging bot or API integration. You can share your clinic's public booking link via WhatsApp manually, but automated reminders are sent via email.

Are reminder emails sent during the 3-month free trial?

Yes. The 3-month free trial includes full platform features, allowing you to test automated reminder delivery during your trial period.

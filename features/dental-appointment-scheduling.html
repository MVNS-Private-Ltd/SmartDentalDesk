Centralized Clinic Calendar & Queue Management

DentalSmart provides a structured web-based appointment calendar designed for independent dental clinics, associate dentists, and reception teams. Instead of juggling physical appointment diaries or detached spreadsheets, dental teams can review upcoming visits, record patient check-ins, and track attendance from any modern web browser.

The scheduling module suits solo practitioners managing their own consultations as well as group practices with dedicated receptionists handling the front-desk patient flow.

Daily Queue & Time Slots

View daily appointment timelines, monitor patient arrivals, and check patient notes and selected services prior to each consultation.

24/7 Public Booking Link

Every clinic receives a dedicated public booking link (/book.html?c=<slug>) allowing patients to request visits 24/7 without calling the clinic.

Patient Attendance & Visit Lifecycle Tracking

Accurate schedule management requires knowing whether a patient is waiting, in the operatory, or missed their slot. DentalSmart tracks appointments through explicit operational statuses:

Pending Scheduled Confirmed In-Progress Completed Cancelled No-Show

When a patient checks in, front-desk staff can update the status to In-Progress so clinical staff know the patient has arrived. Marking visits as Completed feeds directly into patient visit histories and performance reports, giving clinic owners clear visibility into no-show and cancellation rates.

Collaborative Scheduling for Dentists & Front Desk

Dental practices rely on tight coordination between reception staff and treating dentists. DentalSmart offers role-specific views to streamline this handoff:

Front-Desk Receptionists

Manage the incoming booking queue, approve or adjust patient requests, update attendance statuses, and initiate billing upon appointment completion.

Treating Dentists

Review their daily appointment list, access the linked patient medical history and allergy alerts, and log clinical treatment notes directly from the chair.

Plan Availability & Boundaries: Appointment scheduling, calendar management, and public booking links are included on all subscription tiers (Starter, Growth, Premium) and during the active 3-month free trial. “24/7” describes patient availability to submit appointment requests online, not 24/7 round-the-clock live phone support. Multi-chair visual grid routing and third-party calendar synchronizations (e.g. Google Calendar sync) are not currently supported.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do online booking requests reach the clinic schedule?

When a patient submits a booking through your clinic’s public link, the appointment appears in your calendar queue. Clinic staff can review the patient's details and approve or reschedule the booking as needed.

Does scheduling an appointment automatically link to existing patient records?

Yes. When an appointment is booked with an existing patient's phone number or email, DentalSmart automatically associates the visit with their existing profile, avoiding duplicate patient records.

Can we send reminders to reduce missed appointments?

Yes. Clinics on Growth and Premium tiers (or on an active 3-month trial) can utilize automated email appointment reminders sent to patients approximately 24 hours prior to their scheduled consultation.

Itemized Treatment Invoicing for Dental Practices

Dental procedures often involve multiple steps, materials, and clinical sessions. DentalSmart enables clinic staff to create transparent, itemized invoices directly linked to the patient's record and appointment.

Line Items & Quantity Calculations

Add treatments with clear descriptions, procedure quantities, unit prices, and automatic subtotal calculations.

Discounts & Configurable Taxes

Apply flat or percentage discounts to line items or the invoice total, and configure regional tax rates (such as GST or local sales taxes) as needed.

Balance Due Tracking

The system automatically calculates outstanding balances (total amount minus recorded payments), making it easy to identify partial settlements.

Printable & Shareable Receipts

Generate clean, professional receipts for patients to retain for their personal records or reimbursement submissions.

Payment Status Tracking Across Multiple Methods

Dental clinics accept payments across a mix of channels. DentalSmart allows staff to record manual settlements accurately:

Payment Status Lifecycle

Invoices are tracked across clear statuses: Unpaid for pending bills, Partially Paid for installments or treatment advances, and Paid once fully settled.

Supported Payment Methods

Record payments received via Cash, Card (in-clinic POS terminal), UPI (in India), or Direct Bank Transfer, maintaining a clean audit trail.

Billing Boundaries & Scope: DentalSmart provides clinic-managed invoice creation, configurable tax rate fields (e.g., GST percentages for clinics in India), and manual payment recording. DentalSmart is not a tax advisory service and does not guarantee statutory tax compliance or provide automated e-invoicing portal sync (e.g. government IRN/e-way bill generation). Payment recording in DentalSmart reflects settlements confirmed by your clinic; DentalSmart does not operate an embedded online patient merchant checkout or integrate with third-party insurance clearinghouses for automated claim adjudication.

Connecting Billing to Clinic Revenue Reports

Every invoice and recorded payment feeds directly into the clinic's revenue reports. Clinic owners can monitor collected revenue vs. invoiced revenue across custom date ranges, track aging outstanding balances, and evaluate payment method distributions without manual spreadsheet tallying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we configure GST rates for dental treatments in India?

Yes. DentalSmart includes configurable tax fields where Indian clinics can enter their applicable GST percentage (such as 0%, 5%, 12%, or 18%), calculating tax amounts cleanly on the invoice.

Can patients pay invoices in installments?

Yes. When a patient makes a partial payment, you can record the amount received. The invoice updates to Partially Paid and displays the remaining balance due.

Which subscription plans include dental billing?

Invoicing, tax/discount configuration, balance tracking, and payment recording are core features included across all DentalSmart plans (Starter, Growth, and Premium) as well as the active 3-month free trial.

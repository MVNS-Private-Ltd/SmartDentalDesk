Frequently Asked Questions

Can I put my DentalSmart booking link on my existing clinic website?

Yes. You can paste your dedicated booking link into any "Book Appointment" button on your existing clinic website, Instagram bio, or Facebook page.

Does an online booking immediately confirm the visit?

Online requests are submitted into your clinic's queue in a pending status. Front-desk staff can confirm the time slot or contact the patient to adjust timing if needed.

What happens to bookings when the 3-month free trial ends?

Your booking link and patient records remain safely preserved. Simply select a subscription tier to continue accepting new online bookings uninterrupted.

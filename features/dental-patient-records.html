Centralized Patient Profiles & Booking Integration

DentalSmart stores core patient information in a centralized, secure digital record. Each profile records patient contact information (name, phone, email), demographic details (date of birth, gender, address), general medical notes, and medical alerts.

When patients request appointments through your clinic's public booking link, DentalSmart automatically looks up existing patients using their phone number or email address. If an existing record matches, the new appointment is seamlessly linked to their existing history. If no match is found, a new profile is created automatically, preventing fragmented clinic records.

Medical History & Allergy Alerts

Highlight critical drug allergies, systemic health conditions, and medical precautions directly on the patient header before initiating treatment.

Chronological Visit History

Review past consultations, attendance outcomes, linked appointments, and previous treatments in a structured timeline.

Treatment Logs with Tooth-Number Tracking

Clinical documentation must accurately reflect which teeth received treatment and what procedures were completed. DentalSmart treatment records allow dentists to log:

Procedure Descriptions & Costs

Record procedure details (e.g., Composite Restoration, Scaling, Root Canal Treatment), procedure costs, and clinical notes for each session.

Tooth Number Identification

Tag treatments with specific tooth numbers (e.g., FDI or Universal notation in text) to clearly document which quadrant or tooth was treated.

Prescriptions & Clinical Notes

Document prescribed medications, dosage instructions, post-operative recommendations, and clinical observations directly into the record.

Photos & File Attachments

Upload clinical photographs, X-ray image files, and signed consent forms to the patient profile to keep diagnostic documentation organized.

Clinical Capabilities & Boundaries: Patient records, allergy logging, treatment logs with text tooth numbers, and file attachments are included on all subscription plans. Please note that DentalSmart records tooth numbers via structured clinical text fields; it does not include an interactive graphical 3D odontogram, computerized periodontal probing charts (bleeding/pocket depth charting), automated DICOM radiology hardware integration, or automated electronic health record data migration from legacy proprietary desktop systems.

Role Boundaries & Moving Away from Paper Files

Transitioning a dental clinic from paper registers to a cloud-based software system improves record accessibility while maintaining role boundaries:

Front-Desk Reception Team

Receptionists can register new patients, update contact info, check scheduled visits, and generate invoices, while clinical diagnoses and treatment logs remain focused for treating dentists.

Treating Dentists & Associates

Dentists can quickly review past visit notes, tooth numbers, and uploaded X-rays right at the operatory chair without searching through physical filing cabinets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we upload X-rays and patient photos?

Yes. DentalSmart supports uploading standard image and document attachments directly to each patient profile, allowing you to store pre- and post-op photos or digital radiographs.

How are duplicate patient records avoided?

When an appointment is booked through your public link or manually at the front desk, the system checks existing records by phone and email. Matches automatically link to the existing profile.

Can treatments be converted directly into an invoice?

Yes. Logged treatment records can be pulled directly into our dental billing and invoicing module, creating itemized invoices with a single click.

Financial Reports Grounded in Actual Billing

DentalSmart generates practice reports derived directly from your clinic's verified invoicing and appointment logs. Rather than estimating performance, our reports track:

Collected Revenue

Total funds actually received and recorded with a settlement date within the selected reporting period.

Invoiced Revenue

The total value of invoices generated within the timeframe, giving insight into gross treatment volume.

Current Outstanding Balances

Snapshot of all unpaid patient balances and unpaid invoice counts, helping you follow up on pending receivables.

Payment Method Breakdown

Distribution of received payments across Cash, UPI, Card, and Bank Transfer to review payment preferences.

Appointment Volume & Patient Attendance Analytics

Understanding appointment outcomes is vital to managing clinic efficiency and operatory utilization:

Visit Completion Volume

Count of successfully completed consultations and procedures during the reporting timeframe.

Cancellation & No-Show Rates

Calculates the exact percentage of missed or cancelled appointments against resolved visits to evaluate attendance patterns.

New vs. Returning Patients

Identifies how many patients who completed a visit in the period were first-time visitors versus returning clinic patients.

Flexible Date Ranges

Filter data across standard periods: This Month, Last Month, Last 6 Months, Year to Date, or set a Custom Range.

Report Scope & Availability: Practice reports require an active DentalSmart subscription or active trial period. All figures are calculated from your clinic's own recorded appointments and invoices. DentalSmart does not manufacture artificial industry benchmarks, fabricate financial growth projections, or promise specific revenue outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are cancellation and no-show rates calculated?

The rate is calculated as the number of cancelled or no-show visits divided by the total number of resolved visits (completed + cancelled + no-show) in that period.

Are reports updated in real time?

Yes. As soon as an invoice is marked paid or an appointment status is updated, the changes are reflected in your dashboard reports.
